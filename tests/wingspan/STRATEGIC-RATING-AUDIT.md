## Protocol and limitations
Observed legal positions generated by the independent baseline policy. Replay identical prefix, keep vs quarantine a single card (never recycled). Re-shuffle hidden deck/bonus deck and pre-generate future dice per common future seed; both branches use the same seed and same adaptive opponent policy, NOT necessarily identical actions after divergence. Same policy/budget on both branches. The independent baseline plays greedily, not optimally; negative marginal losses are possible because policy changes and randomness affect continuation. The estimate is conditional on this policy and fixture, NOT an oracle of optimal play. No fixed opponent trajectory, all-powers simulation, multi-player or six-agent tournament certification. One observed seed can generate multiple correlated positions; confidence intervals require clustering by game seed and additional independent validation. Critical decision error is regret >=5 expected points; safe-loss proxy is a numeric rating <3 with estimated loss >=5. Ties broken by id; unambiguous worst requires gap > max(1,1.96*combined SE), a heuristic because paired loss covariance is not measured. Top-3 measure only for >3 cards and separated boundary.
